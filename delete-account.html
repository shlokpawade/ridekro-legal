Delete My Account

You have the right to permanently delete your Ridekro account and associated personal data. Here's how.

⚠️ This action is permanent. Once your account is deleted, your profile, ride history, and personal data cannot be recovered. Please read this page carefully before submitting a deletion request.

1 What Gets Deleted

When your account deletion request is processed, the following data is permanently removed from our systems:

  • Your name, profile information, and profile photo
  • Your registered phone number and account credentials
  • Your email address (if provided)
  • Your saved emergency contact (name, phone number, and email)
  • Saved addresses and location preferences
  • In-app ratings and feedback you've given or received
  • Push notification tokens associated with your device
  • Wallet balance and transaction history (subject to financial retention rules below)
  • Driver profile data (for driver accounts): vehicle details, Aadhaar, PAN, and driving licence uploads, verification history

2 Data Retention Policy

Some data must be retained after account deletion to comply with Indian legal and financial regulations, or to resolve outstanding disputes. This data is anonymised or pseudonymised where possible.

Data Type Action on Deletion Reason
Name, phone number, profile Deleted within 30 days No legal requirement to retain
Ride history (routes, timestamps) Retained for 3 years Dispute resolution and legal compliance
Payment transaction records Retained for 7 years Indian financial regulations (GST, audit)
Driver documents (Aadhaar, PAN, licence) Retained for 1 year post-closure Regulatory compliance and dispute resolution
Safety incident reports Retained as required by law Legal obligation and user safety
Device logs and crash reports Deleted within 30 days No legal requirement to retain

Retained data is stored securely, access-restricted, and used only for the stated legal purposes. It is not used for marketing or shared with third parties.

3 How to Delete Your Account

There are two ways to delete your Ridekro account:

Option 1 — In-App (Recommended): Go to your Profile screen in the Ridekro app → scroll to the bottom → tap "Delete Account". Follow the on-screen confirmation steps. This is the fastest way to submit a deletion request.

If you cannot access the app, use the email method below:

  1. Compose an email Open your email app and create a new email to pawadeshlok@gmail.com
  2. Use the correct subject line Subject: Account Deletion Request – [Your Registered Phone Number]
  3. Include required information Your registered phone number, your account type (passenger or driver), the reason for deletion (optional), and confirmation that you understand the action is permanent.
  4. Verify your identity We may send an OTP to your registered number to confirm your identity before processing the deletion.
  5. Confirmation & processing We'll confirm receipt within 48 hours and complete the deletion within 30 days.

4 Email Template

You can use the template below. Just fill in your details and send it to pawadeshlok@gmail.com.

5 After Your Account is Deleted

  • You will receive a confirmation email once your account has been deleted
  • You will no longer be able to log in to Ridekro with your phone number
  • Any pending earnings (for drivers) will be processed before deletion — ensure your bank details are up to date
  • Active rides must be completed before a deletion request can be processed
  • If you wish to use Ridekro again in the future, you will need to create a new account

6 Questions?

If you have questions about the deletion process or your data rights, contact us at pawadeshlok@gmail.com. You can also read our full Privacy Policy to understand how we handle your data.
