1 About Ridekro

Ridekro is a technology platform operated by Ridekro Technologies that connects riders seeking transportation with independent drivers offering ride services. Ridekro is a marketplace platform — we are not a transportation company and do not employ drivers.

Drivers on Ridekro are independent service providers. The transportation contract is between the rider and the driver. Ridekro facilitates the connection, payment processing, and platform infrastructure.

2 Eligibility & Account Registration

To use Ridekro, you must:

  • Be at least 18 years of age
  • Have a valid Indian mobile phone number
  • Provide accurate and truthful registration information
  • Not have a previously suspended or banned Ridekro account

You are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your account. Any activity that occurs under your account is your responsibility. Notify us immediately at pawadeshlok@gmail.com if you suspect unauthorised access.

Ridekro reserves the right to refuse registration or suspend accounts that provide false information or violate these terms.

3 Rider Responsibilities

As a rider on Ridekro, you agree to:

  • Provide accurate pickup and drop-off locations when booking a ride
  • Be ready at the pickup location within a reasonable time after the driver arrives
  • Treat drivers with respect and courtesy at all times
  • Not request rides for illegal purposes or to transport prohibited items
  • Pay the fare as displayed in the app, including any applicable surge pricing
  • Not attempt to manipulate the fare, route, or payment system
  • Provide honest ratings and feedback after each ride
  • Not share your account with others or allow minors to use your account unsupervised
  • Report any safety concerns or incidents to Ridekro support promptly
Cancellation Policy: Repeated ride cancellations after driver acceptance may result in a temporary booking restriction. Excessive cancellations may lead to account suspension.

4 Driver Responsibilities & Verification

As a driver partner on Ridekro, you agree to:

  • Hold a valid driving licence appropriate for the vehicle you operate
  • Maintain valid vehicle registration and all required permits under Indian law
  • Keep your vehicle in a safe, roadworthy condition at all times
  • Accept and complete rides in good faith once accepted
  • Follow the route suggested by the app or the most reasonable route to the destination
  • Treat passengers with respect and maintain professional conduct
  • Not drive under the influence of alcohol, drugs, or any substance that impairs judgment
  • Not use a mobile phone while driving (except hands-free navigation)
  • Provide accurate vehicle and location information at all times
  • Not charge fares beyond what is displayed in the Ridekro app
  • Comply with all applicable traffic laws and regulations

4.1 KYC Verification Requirement

All drivers must complete identity verification (KYC) before going online and accepting rides. The following documents are required:

  • Profile photo
  • Aadhaar card (image upload)
  • PAN card (image upload)
  • Driving licence (image upload)
  • Vehicle type and registration number

After submission, your account status becomes under review. You cannot go online or accept rides until your documents are reviewed and your account is approved. Ridekro reserves the right to reject or suspend driver accounts that submit false, expired, or unverifiable documents.

Drivers acknowledge that they are independent contractors and not employees of Ridekro Technologies. Ridekro does not guarantee a minimum number of rides or earnings.

5 Commission & Dues System

Ridekro charges a platform commission on each completed ride. The commission rate is communicated to drivers during onboarding and may be updated with prior notice.

5.1 How Commission Works

  • For each completed ride, Ridekro deducts its platform commission from the total fare
  • The remaining amount is credited to the driver's earnings balance
  • Drivers can view their earnings, commission deductions, and payout history in the Driver Dashboard

5.2 Commission Dues

  • If a driver's commission dues exceed a defined threshold, their ability to accept new rides may be temporarily restricted until dues are cleared
  • Ridekro reserves the right to adjust commission rates with a minimum of 7 days' notice to active drivers
  • Disputes regarding commission calculations must be raised within 15 days of the relevant ride date

5.3 Payouts

  • Driver earnings are processed via bank transfer to the registered bank account
  • Payout schedules and minimum withdrawal amounts are communicated within the driver section of the Ridekro app
  • Ridekro is not liable for delays caused by banking systems or incorrect bank details provided by the driver

6 Payments & Fare Policy

All payments on Ridekro are processed through Razorpay, a licensed payment aggregator in India. By using Ridekro, you also agree to Razorpay's terms of service.

6.1 Fare Calculation

  • Fares are calculated based on distance, time, vehicle type, and applicable surge pricing
  • The estimated fare shown before booking is an approximation; the final fare may vary based on actual route and time
  • Surge pricing may apply during high-demand periods and will be clearly indicated before booking

6.2 Payment Methods

  • Ridekro accepts UPI, debit/credit cards, net banking, and supported digital wallets via Razorpay
  • Cash payments may be available in select areas as indicated in the app

6.3 Refunds

  • Refunds for cancelled rides (where applicable) are processed within 5–7 business days to the original payment method
  • Refund eligibility is determined by Ridekro's cancellation policy at the time of the ride
  • Disputes regarding charges must be raised within 7 days of the ride via pawadeshlok@gmail.com
Payment Disclaimer: Ridekro is not a bank or financial institution. We do not hold funds on your behalf beyond what is necessary to process a transaction. All payment processing is handled by Razorpay under their regulatory compliance framework.

7 Fraud & Misuse Policy

The following actions are strictly prohibited on the Ridekro platform and may result in immediate account suspension and legal action:

  • Creating fake accounts or using another person's identity
  • Manipulating GPS location to falsify ride routes or distances
  • Colluding with another user to generate fraudulent rides or payments
  • Attempting to reverse, chargeback, or dispute legitimate payments
  • Using the platform to harass, threaten, or harm other users
  • Attempting to access or tamper with Ridekro's systems, APIs, or databases
  • Submitting false or forged documents during driver registration
  • Offering or accepting payments outside the Ridekro platform to avoid commission

Ridekro actively monitors for fraudulent activity. Accounts found engaging in fraud will be permanently banned and may be reported to law enforcement authorities.

8 Safety & Emergency SOS

Ridekro includes safety features such as driver verification, OTP ride confirmation, real-time tracking, and an in-app SOS emergency tool. However:

  • Ridekro does not guarantee the safety of any ride or the conduct of any driver or passenger
  • Users are responsible for their own safety and should exercise reasonable caution
  • In case of an emergency during a ride, contact local emergency services (100 / 112) immediately
  • Report any safety incidents to Ridekro at pawadeshlok@gmail.com within 24 hours

8.1 SOS Feature

Passengers can activate the SOS feature during an active ride. When triggered after a 5-second countdown:

  • An emergency alert email is automatically sent to your saved emergency contact via Resend, including your live GPS location, driver name, driver phone number, and vehicle details
  • The app opens the native SMS composer with a pre-filled emergency message for you to send manually to your emergency contact
  • The SOS event is logged in our system and is visible to our admin team

The SOS feature requires a saved emergency contact (name, phone, and email) in your profile and active location permission. Ridekro is not a substitute for emergency services. Always call 112 in a life-threatening situation.

Ridekro is not liable for accidents, injuries, property damage, or losses that occur during a ride. The transportation service is provided by independent drivers, not by Ridekro Technologies.
